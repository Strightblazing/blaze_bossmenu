**[FREE] Blaze Boss Menu · boss menu, applications, time clock & contracts for any job**

A drop-in replacement for **qbx_management** (GPL-3.0 fork). Keeps its tables, exports and events. Built for **Qbox** (QBCore works too) with **ox_lib**, optional **Lation UI**.

**Features**
• Staff list (online + offline) with callsigns, hours and badges
• Hire nearby or by ID, promote, demote, fire (checked on the server)
• Profiles & `/myjob`, commendations, write-ups, probation
• Employee of the Week, announcements, activity log
• Funds, bonuses, payroll, per-rank withdraw limits
• Rank editor, gang menus, radial **Work** entry
• Job applications with an in-game form builder
• Review points, team votes, quick replies, interview check-in
• `/applicationstatus` and a `/jobs` "Now hiring" board
• Time clock: tap, swipe or PIN, live shift timer, next rank progress
• Time clock styles: full, standard or minimal
• Employment contracts signed with the mouse, per-job templates
• Duty points, job outfits and wardrobes, starter items
• `/bossmenuadmin` admin menu; the boss menu stays roleplay only
• Place points in game with a live preview: invisible, a PED or a prop
• `/bossdebug` live panel (move + resize), `/bossmenutest` with **View as**
• Auto-detects ox_target / qb-target, illenium-appearance, ox_inventory, popular phones and banking
• Discord logs (webhook only in server.cfg)
• …and many more!
