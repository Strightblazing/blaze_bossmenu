**[FREE] Blaze Boss Menu · boss menu, applications, time clock & contracts for any job**

A drop-in replacement for **qbx_management** (GPL-3.0 fork of it). Keeps its tables, exports and events, and adds a lot more. Built for **Qbox** (QBCore works too) with **ox_lib**, optional **Lation UI**.

**Boss menu**
• Staff list (online + offline) with callsigns, hours, badges
• Hire nearby or by ID, promote, demote, fire: rank rules checked on the server
• Profiles & `/myjob`, commendations / write-ups, probation, Employee of the Week, announcements, activity log
• Funds, bonuses, payroll, per-rank limits · rank editor · gang menus · radial **Work** entry

**Applications**
• Application points + in-game form builder · review points, team votes, quick replies
• Interview invites with check-in · `/applicationstatus` · `/jobs` "Now hiring" board

**Time clock**
• Tap your virtual ID, swipe it, or type your PIN
• Live shift timer, weekly hours, next rank with pay, who's on duty
• Full / standard / minimal styles, adjustable size

**Employment contract**
• New hires sign with the mouse; job, rank, pay and manager filled in · per-job templates

**Setup & admin**
• Place every point in game (aim + E); managers move their own · Show zones
• `/bossdebug` live panel · `/bossmenutest` with **View as** any rank
• Auto-detects ox_target / qb-target, illenium-appearance, ox_inventory, popular phones and banking
• Discord logs (webhook only in server.cfg)
